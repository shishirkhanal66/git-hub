CEO OF SHTYOO

Shisir Khanal

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Shisir Khanal is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of SHTYOO, a modern fashion-focused e-commerce platform launched in 2026. His vision for SHTYOO is to create a trusted online destination where men can easily discover stylish clothing, shop with confidence, and enjoy a seamless digital shopping experience.

Driven by a passion for innovation and customer satisfaction, he believes that fashion should be accessible, convenient, and affordable for everyone. Through SHTYOO, he continues to focus on delivering quality products, excellent service, and a shopping experience that reflects the needs of today's generation.

About the CEO

Shisir Khanal is a Nepali entrepreneur, educator, and leader who has spent years working on initiatives that create meaningful impact. Throughout his career, he has been involved in projects focused on leadership, education, innovation, and organizational development. His work has consistently reflected a commitment to empowering people and building solutions that create long-term value.

In 2026, he founded SHTYOO with a simple idea: to make online fashion shopping easier, more reliable, and more enjoyable for men. What started as a vision to provide quality clothing through a customer-first platform has grown into a brand focused on style, convenience, and trust.

As CEO, he oversees the company's strategic direction, growth, and customer experience. He is dedicated to building a platform that not only offers fashionable clothing but also creates lasting relationships with customers through quality, transparency, and service.

His leadership is guided by innovation, integrity, and continuous improvement. By combining modern technology with a strong understanding of customer needs, he continues to lead SHTYOO toward becoming one of Nepal's most trusted destinations for men's fashion and lifestyle products.
